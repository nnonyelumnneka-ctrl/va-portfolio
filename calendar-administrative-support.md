# Calendar & Administrative Support Portfolio Project

## Project Overview

This is a sample calendar and administrative support project created to demonstrate my ability to organize appointments, manage schedules, prioritize tasks, and maintain clear administrative records.

## Project Scenario

A business owner has several meetings and appointments during the week and needs help organizing the schedule while avoiding conflicts.

## Sample Weekly Schedule

| Day | Time | Activity | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | 9:00 AM | Team Meeting | High |
| Monday | 2:00 PM | Client Follow-Up | High |
| Tuesday | 11:00 AM | Administrative Review | Medium |
| Wednesday | 10:00 AM | Client Meeting | High |
| Thursday | 1:00 PM | Project Planning | Medium |
| Friday | 10:00 AM | Weekly Review | High |

## Administrative Process

1. Review the appointments and tasks.
2. Identify important and time-sensitive activities.
3. Check for scheduling conflicts.
4. Organize appointments clearly.
5. Add relevant reminders.
6. Confirm important appointments.
7. Follow up on outstanding tasks.
8. Keep the calendar updated when changes occur.
