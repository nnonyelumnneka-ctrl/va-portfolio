# Data Entry Portfolio Project

## Project Overview

This is a sample data entry project created to demonstrate my ability to organize information accurately, work with spreadsheets, maintain records, and pay attention to detail.

## Project Scenario

A small business provided a list of customer information that needed to be organized into a clear and easy-to-use spreadsheet.

The information included customer names, contact details, order status, and payment status.

## Data Entry Process

1. Review the information provided.
2. Enter the information accurately into the spreadsheet.
3. Organize the information into appropriate columns.
4. Check for missing or incorrect information.
5. Keep the formatting consistent.
6. Review the completed spreadsheet for errors.
7. Save and organize the final document.
