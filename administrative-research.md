# Administrative Research Portfolio Project

## Project Overview

This is a sample administrative research project created to demonstrate my ability to gather information, organize findings, verify relevant details, and present information clearly for administrative and business purposes.

## Project Scenario

A small business owner wants to identify potential suppliers for office stationery.

My task is to research potential suppliers and organize the information in a simple format that makes comparison easier.

## Research Information

| Supplier | Product Category | Location | Contact Method | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ABC Office Supplies | Stationery | Lagos | Email | Offers general office supplies |
| Prime Stationery | Office Equipment | Abuja | Website | Offers office equipment and stationery |
| Bright Office Hub | Stationery | Port Harcourt | Phone/Email | Supplies businesses and schools |

## Research Process

1. Understand the information required.
2. Identify relevant sources.
3. Gather the required information.
4. Organize the findings in a spreadsheet or table.
5. Check the information for accuracy and completeness.
6. Highlight useful observations.
7. Present the findings clearly for easy decision-making.

## Sample Research Summary

Based on the information gathered, the suppliers offer different categories of office products and operate in different locations.

Before selecting a supplier, the business should confirm current prices, availability, delivery options, payment terms, and other relevant conditions directly with the supplier.
