# Customer Support Portfolio Project

## Project Overview

This is a sample customer support project created to demonstrate my ability to communicate professionally with customers, understand their concerns, provide clear responses, and identify the appropriate next steps.

## Customer Enquiry

"I ordered a laptop three days ago and I have not received it. Please can you tell me what is happening with my order?"

## My Response

Hello,

Thank you for reaching out. I'm sorry for the inconvenience and understand how frustrating it can be to wait for an order without receiving an update.

I would be happy to help check the status of your order. Please send your order number so I can review the available information and provide you with the appropriate next step.

Thank you for your patience.

Best regards,  
Cynthia
