# Generative AI & AI Prompting Portfolio Project

## Project Overview

This is a sample Generative AI and AI prompting project created to demonstrate my ability to use AI tools to support administrative work, customer service, content creation, research, and professional communication.

## Project Scenario

A business receives a customer complaint and needs a professional response. I use a structured prompt to guide an AI tool in creating a suitable draft, then review and edit the result before using it.

## Sample Prompt

Create a professional and empathetic customer support response to a customer whose order has been delayed.

The response should:

- Acknowledge the customer's concern.
- Apologize for the inconvenience without making unsupported promises.
- Ask for the information needed to investigate the issue.
- Clearly explain the next step.
- Use a polite and professional tone.

## AI-Assisted Draft

Hello,

Thank you for contacting us. We understand your concern regarding the delay with your order, and we apologize for the inconvenience.

Please provide your order number so we can review the available information and determine the appropriate next step.

Thank you for your patience and understanding.

Best regards,
Customer Support Team

## My Review Process

AI-generated content should not automatically be used without review.

I review the response for:

- Accuracy
- Tone
- Clarity
- Grammar
- Relevance
- Missing information
- Unsupported claims

I then edit the response where necessary before presenting the final version.
