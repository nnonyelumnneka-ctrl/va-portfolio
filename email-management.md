# Email Management Portfolio Project

## Project Overview

This is a sample email management project created to demonstrate my ability to organize emails, identify priorities, communicate professionally, and keep track of important customer and administrative messages.

## Scenario

A busy business owner receives several emails throughout the day, including customer enquiries, urgent requests, newsletters, appointment requests, and general business messages.

My task is to organize the inbox and identify which messages require immediate attention.

## Email Prioritization

### High Priority

- Customer complaints that require immediate attention
- Urgent business requests
- Time-sensitive appointment or meeting requests
- Issues that may affect a customer's order or service

### Medium Priority

- General customer enquiries
- Follow-up requests
- Non-urgent administrative messages
- Appointment confirmations

### Low Priority

- Newsletters
- Promotional emails
- General updates that do not require immediate action

## Sample Professional Email Response

Subject: Follow-Up Regarding Your Request

Hello,

Thank you for reaching out.

I have received your message and will review the information provided. I will get back to you with an update as soon as possible.

Thank you for your patience.

Best regards,  
Cynthia

## Email Management Process

1. Review incoming emails.
2. Identify urgent and time-sensitive messages.
3. Categorize emails according to priority.
4. Respond to messages that require immediate attention.
5. Flag messages that require follow-up.
6. Archive or organize completed conversations.
7. Review outstanding emails to ensure nothing important is missed.
